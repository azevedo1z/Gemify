# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Gemify is a personal finance app for Android. This policy describes what it does with your data.

## Data the app stores

Gemify stores only what you type: your monthly income, your expenses (name, amount, installments and date), the month references and a log of the changes you make. Everything is kept in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. The app also remembers your theme choice (light, dark or system) in the same private storage.

## Data the app collects or shares

None. Gemify has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting, and release builds do not request the internet permission. Nothing you type leaves your phone unless you choose to.

## When data leaves your phone

Only through actions you start:

- **Share** sends a text summary of a month to the app you pick in the Android share sheet.
- **Export backup** writes a JSON file with all your data to the location you pick. The file is not encrypted; anyone with access to it can read your finances.

## Backups and device transfer

Android automatic backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Gemify. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases everything, so keep an exported backup somewhere safe.
